Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to EduLearn. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data collected from students, instructors, and visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your information when utilizing our web platform and educational services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide effective learning experiences, including:

  • Account Information: First name, last name, username, and verified email address upon registration.
  • Profile Details: Optional biography, profile picture, phone number, and specialization (for instructors).
  • Academic & Enrollment Data: Courses enrolled in, course progress, completed lessons, and inquiry messages sent to instructors.
  • Payment & Order References: Razorpay order ID, payment transaction ID, and timestamp.

3. Payment Protection & Razorpay Integration

Bank-Grade Payment Security: EduLearn does not store, process, or view credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs. All payment transactions are handled directly through Razorpay with 256-bit SSL encryption.

Payment details are transmitted securely to payment gateways under strict compliance with RBI standards and PCI-DSS requirements.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use collected information strictly to:

  • Provide authenticated access to course content and lecture materials.
  • Dispatch enrollment confirmations and payment receipts via email.
  • Facilitate direct communication between enrolled students and course instructors.
  • Ensure account security via 6-digit one-time password (OTP) verification.

5. Data Retention & Sharing

We never sell or lease personal information to third parties or advertisers. Data is stored on secured servers and retained only as long as your account remains active or as required by statutory regulations.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data updates, please contact our support team.
