Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I purchase and access a course?

Browse our course catalog, click Add to Cart or Enroll Now, and proceed to checkout. Payments are processed securely via Razorpay. Once verified, your course immediately appears in your Dashboard, and you receive an email confirmation.

2. How can I contact an instructor if I have questions?

Visit the Instructors Page, choose your instructor, and click Contact Instructor. You can send questions directly, and view delivery and read receipts in your Dashboard → Sent Messages tab.

3. What should I do if I forgot my password?

Go to the Forgot Password page, enter your registered email address, and you will receive a 6-digit verification OTP to securely set a new password.

4. Are courses self-paced?

Yes! All enrolled courses on EduLearn provide lifetime access to videos, lecture notes, and downloadable materials so you can learn at your own pace anytime, anywhere.
